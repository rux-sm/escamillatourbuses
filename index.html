How booking works

Read the answers to common questions
  1. 1

    Ask for a quote

    Tell us where you’re going, when, and how many are coming. We price by the trip’s miles and days and reply with a written quote.

  2. 2

    Hold your date

    The quote holds the bus for a week. Sign the quote and our terms, and send a 20% down payment or your purchase order.

  3. 3

    Send your itinerary

    Send your times, your stops and two trip contacts, and pay the balance a week before you leave.
